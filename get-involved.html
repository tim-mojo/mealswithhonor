Get Involved

Two hours a week covers a route.

Volunteers are the program. Money buys the food; people deliver the part that matters.

Volunteer

What a route asks of you

A standing two-hour block, once a week, on the same route. You’ll pick up prepared meals from the community kitchen, drive six to nine stops, and take the time to actually talk to people at each one.

  • A valid driver's license, insurance, and a car that fits four meal crates.
  • A background check, which we run and pay for.
  • One 90-minute orientation before your first solo route.
  • A commitment of at least three months, so households aren't meeting someone new every few weeks.

Other Ways to Help

Not everyone can drive a route

Kitchen shifts

Prep and portioning, Monday and Wednesday mornings. No driving, no set commitment — come when you can.

Run a food drive

We'll send the current needs list, printable signage, and arrange pickup. Workplaces and congregations run most of ours.

Skilled volunteering

Bookkeeping, grant writing, vehicle maintenance, and photography are all things we currently pay for and would rather not.

Donate

What your giving covers

Donations fund food, fuel, and the kitchen. Volunteers cover the rest, which is why the cost per meal stays where it is.

  • $25 — a week of meals for one household.
  • $100 — a month of meals for one household.
  • $600 — fuel and supplies for one route for a year.

Figures above are placeholders. No payment processor has been selected for this site yet — this section is layout only, and the button below opens an email until one is in place.

Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. A copy of our current financial statement is available by writing to Meals With Honor Inc., Pasadena, MD. Documents and information submitted under the Maryland Solicitations Act are also available, for the cost of postage and copies, from the Maryland Secretary of State, State House, Annapolis, MD 21401.
