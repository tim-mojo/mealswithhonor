    Lunches & Dinners

    Currently we use a sign-up sheet for those requesting meals. They complete the form, we confirm the pick-up date and time by email and/or a phone call, and they pick up their meal.

    • Complete one simple form to request meals.
    • We confirm your pick-up date and time by email and/or phone.
    • We don’t have a regular delivery route, but we make large deliveries to Evolve Life Center, Arundel House of Hope, and volunteers who distribute directly to the unhoused.
    • No cost, ever.

    Volunteer Support

    Our volunteers do far more than hand out food. They:

    • Prepare and package meals for distribution or pick-up.
    • Help identify those who may need our service.
    • Assist with fundraising.
    • Help secure additional resources that can provide additional support.
