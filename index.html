About Us

More than a meal

Meals with Honor began with three volunteers with the desire to feed those in need, working out of a commercial kitchen donated by Truck of Deliciousness. We serve anyone in need: those who are homeless, veterans, the elderly, and families who are struggling. There are no hoops to jump through to receive a meal.

Learn More About Us

What We Do

The ways we show up

Meals with Honor fights food insecurity and isolation in our community by distributing meals, building volunteer relationships, and creating spaces where people managing PTSD find connection and support.

Lunches & Dinners

Hot, balanced meals distributed on a set monthly schedule. No cost, and no paperwork.

Volunteer Support

Our volunteers help prepare and package meals, and arrange for pick-up and delivery.

Social & Peer Support

We provide additional support and resources to those in need.

Who We Serve

Neighbors, not case numbers

Households facing food insecurity

For veterans or those on fixed incomes, a hard month can mean skipped meals. We can help fill in the gap by providing two meals a month.
